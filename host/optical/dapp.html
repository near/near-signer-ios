NEAR Signer in a near-connect dapp

A minimal dapp using @hot-labs/near-connect with the NEAR Signer camera executor registered as a debug wallet. Serve this folder over http://localhost (the executor is loaded from ./near-signer-qr-executor.js) and have the app open on the phone.

The deployment is a delegate action with contract-like bytes (about three times compressible, as wasm is), signed on the phone and returned here, never sent: it shows a request of many QR codes.
